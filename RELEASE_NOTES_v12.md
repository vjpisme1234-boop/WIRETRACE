## Store description / support page

**Point your camera at the print.** Photograph a wire schematic or import one
from your files, and WireTrace AI reads the drawing — components, conductors,
and the printed wire numbers — and hands back the connection list.

**It reads out loud.** Each connection is spoken, so you can stay on the
ladder with your hands on the conductor instead of on a phone. Say "next" to
advance without touching the screen.

**Work in your own order.** Wires come back in the drawing's order, which is
rarely the order you install in. Swipe a wire to add it to a custom reading
order and drag the rows to suit the job.

**Symbols, when the print assumes you already know.** Search the symbol
dictionary, or photograph a symbol you don't recognize and have it identified.
A standards reference is included.

**High Detail mode** for crowded prints. Reads the drawing in three passes —
components, then conductors, then the printed numbers — and reports how many
numbers it found and how many it placed. Slower and costs more per scan, so
Standard stays the default.

**English and Spanish**, including the symbol dictionary.

**20 free scans.** After that, add your own Google Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or
OpenRouter key in Settings and scan without limit — the AI provider bills you
directly. A Gemini key takes about a minute to create at
aistudio.google.com/apikey.
